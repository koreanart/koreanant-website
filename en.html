About

A Kyoto-based gallery introducing Korean art and the beauty of East Asia.

KOREAN ART specializes in antiques, antique ceramics, tea utensils, hanging scrolls, paintings, and modern and contemporary art.
We carefully convey the appeal of each work and provide a trusted art experience for customers in Japan and abroad.
